Method

How the number is built

The position

$100 ÷ $12 = 8.3333 shares at the IPO. Six stock splits multiply that by 480, giving 4,000 shares held continuously.

Each year's value is 4,000 × split-adjusted close.

2000 2:12001 2:12006 2:1 2007 3:22021 4:12024 10:1

Why not use published adjusted prices

Providers round split-adjusted prices to two decimals. In 1999 that price was $0.0978 — published as $0.09 or $0.10, a swing of more than 10%.

Every value here starts from the unadjusted close and divides by the split factor still outstanding at that date, preserving full precision.

Scope

Price return only. Dividends, taxes, fees and any reinvestment are excluded — NVIDIA's dividend is immaterial to a chart with this shape, but the omission should be stated.

2026 is not a year-end value. Label it 2026 — TODAY.

The four eras

1999–2011 Graphics · 2012–2019 GPU computing · 2020–2022 Data-center platform · 2023–2026 AI infrastructure.

These make the natural chapter breaks for the scroll.

Annual returns show the market's reaction around each period — they are not a causal measurement of the event. Price moves for earnings, valuation, macro conditions and expectations at the same time. The 2012 row is the clearest proof: the year modern AI was born, the stock fell.
